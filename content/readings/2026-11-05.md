A woman named Rhoda. A fiery preacher, passionate in every message but then she lost her voice. Doctors said she needed surgery. She refused. “God gave me this voice,” she said, “and He alone will preserve it.” One Sunday, during worship, she knelt at the altar, hit her head to the floor in tears and that was the moment her healing began. Today, she preaches louder and stronger than ever.

God doesn't consult people to fulfill His purpose for you. His plan is sovereign, unique, and perfectly timed. At the pool of Bethesda, a man had been paralyzed for 38 years. Everyone passed him by. But when Jesus showed up, He bypassed tradition and healed him instantly. Why? Because it was time for God's agenda in his life to manifest.

Maybe you've been overlooked, silenced, or pushed aside. Maybe others have stepped in before you repeatedly. But hear this: When it's your divine moment, no one can take your place.

God's agenda for your life cannot be erased by delay, sickness, or people's opinions. He formed you with purpose and when it's time, nothing can stop it.
