Many believers either do not know that this life is meant to be enjoyed or do not believe in enjoying life here. Amazingly, our text reveals that the Lord Jesus, in the midst of the challenges he faced, enjoyed life. He ate and drank well; visited the beach a number of times, attended marriage ceremonies and religious festivals, shared private moments with little children and never worried about anything. But today, the story is different. Many believers do not have any agenda to have fun. In fact, most of the time, they are so serious that they don't even have time to laugh. They do not know that studies have proven that when you laugh, it is a great sign that you're enjoying life. Of course, the Bible teaches that a cheerful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bone.
To enjoy life to the fullest, we need to consciously engage ourselves with activities that recharge our emotions. We need the understanding that enjoying life does not necessarily require huge resources. If we wait for all our problems to be solved before we start enjoying life, it will never happen. Therefore, whether you have little or much, you can enjoy life if you sincerely want. Remember, He came that you might have life to the fullest. Enjoy your life today; it is God's precious gift to you.
