We have learnt over the years that there is time to work and time to rest. Most people out of sheer carelessness misuse their opportunities. Soon after, they learn that most opportunities lost cannot be recovered. Any time you have to do anything, do it without fail. God rewards our work in His own way.
Procrastination is the mother of failure. It can simply be explained as the deliberate dodging of a task when it is needed to be done and accomplished on time. There may be plausible reasons for non-performance but as long as performance is deferred, the task remains. One of the reasons though not often expressed is fear of failure. We feel we need more information to get the task done properly. But the best time to begin is now. If you want all the materials before you start off that house, you may wait for eternity. Part of our text reads, "Don't be afraid, for I am with you! Don't be frightened, for I am your God! I will strengthen you, yes, I help you - yes, and I will uphold you with my saving right hand!"
Another reason for one not doing one's work right away is the trait of excessive perfectionism. Mistake is natural. That is why after making a pencil; they put an eraser at the reverse end. Every person makes a mistake at one point or another. Christ purposely came for those who have made mistakes. Also some are unable to do anything because of lack of focus. Paul states, "This one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind" [Philippians 3:13"]. Get focused on what you are doing. You cannot be everywhere at the same time. Finish what you have at hand; another opportunity will come for the other task. The time to work is now.
