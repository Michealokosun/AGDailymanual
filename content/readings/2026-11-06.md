Modelling is fast becoming a very lucrative business. Not a few movie and sports stars are currently trailing blazes in the fashion and advertising industries as models. A lot of young people look up to them for dressing patterns and hairstyles. Unfortunately, we do not see the same trend in relation to matters of behaviour.
In our society today, you scarcely see men and women who are standing tall as models of good conduct. In some places, medical doctors with cigarettes warn patients to beware of smoking; some parents who are known members of secret societies shamelessly caution their children to avoid cultism in school. And many pastors and politicians are not helping matters.
But thank God for Christianity. Our Lord Jesus Christ left a good example for every believer to follow. He did not teach us to do things He did not practice. His life was a perfect example of His teachings.
He left His throne in heaven, humbled Himself, and died the cruel death on the cross. He paid the ultimate price for our sins and redeemed us. He was unjustly punished, yet, He endured the punishment without complaints. He willingly forgave those that crucified Him.
We are enjoined today, to pattern our lives after that of our Lord Jesus. We should follow His steps in humility, sacrifice, love, advocacy for social justice and model the same for our world.
