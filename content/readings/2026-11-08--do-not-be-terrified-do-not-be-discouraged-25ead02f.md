### KEY VERSE: "...Be strong and courageous. Do not be terrified; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go." (Joshua 1:9, NIV).

### MESSAGE:

There comes a time in life when one will have the opportunity to assume a new responsibility. This new position could come by promotion, selection or election. Stepping into a higher office is not easy. You have not been there before and somehow you are not sure that you can handle it. In times like this, you need to be strong and courageous. You do not have to be afraid or discouraged. One sure way to be strong and courageous is to know that God is with you as you depend on Him always.

As a believer, you have an advantage of divine assistance. Are you occupying an important position in the Church, in the market place, in a tertiary institution; are you a top government officer, a Chief Executive Officer of a bank or a labour leader? Whatever new assignment you are being entrusted with; you can be sure that God will always be there to help you.

Wherever you are, whatever you are called to do, the key to your success is to be strong and courageous, to be guided by the word of God, and to cultivate the attitude of seeking the presence of God. This was exactly God's challenge to Joshua as he prepares to lead His people to the Promised Land.

### CONTROLLING THOUGHT:

The strength and courage for every new assignment is found in God.

### PRAYER:

I look up to You for the strength and courage that I need for the tasks ahead of me today.
