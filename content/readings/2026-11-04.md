What is prayer? Prayer is a channel employed by believers to communicate with God. It is an acceptance or recognition of a need that requires superior power or assistance to handle. It should be the first and only resort when one is at crossroads.
Psalm 35, is one of the imprecatory psalms in the Scripture. Imprecatory psalms are those where the petitioner enjoins God to fight and deal with enemies. In this situation, the petitioner considers himself/herself helpless and appeals to the Supreme Being for deliverance and help.
Are you in a difficult situation now? Are too many forces, physical and spiritual, currently fighting you? Is your marriage delayed or crashing? Is sickness ravaging your home? Are your finances, business and sources of income, etc., closing down? Pray! Pray! Pray!
Take all your burdens to Jehovah, He knows what to do. Jesus cares about you. He can deliver you. Stop complaining and trying to fight back or defend yourself. You cannot save or help yourself. Pray about them all.
