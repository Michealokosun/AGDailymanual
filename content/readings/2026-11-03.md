Fasting is staying away or separating from worldly pleasures to draw near to God in fellowship. Fasting is not mere abstinence from food and water but from worldly pleasures that constitute a distraction from sweet communion with God. These worldly distractions can come from economic or social activities such as work, business engagements, visits from friends, other social gatherings. It can even be from TV, cell phones and the likes.
In Isaiah 58, God told Israel that their fasts did not move Him because they were not done like people who were in fellowship with God. They quarreled and fought even while on fast; they exploited their workers on the day of their fast. Are we doing a different thing today? Some husbands and wives have had cause to slug it out even when they are fasting and praying. Some women have maltreated their housemaids, denied them food and hindered them from going to school while still participating in the Church's praying and fasting program.
For our fasting and praying to yield fruit, we should ensure that our heart is right with God and free from worldly concerns. Beyond mere abstinence from food and water, God recommends, in addition, and more importantly, that we practice justice, free the oppressed, care for the less privileged, show hospitality and brotherly love (Isa 58:6-7). Whenever you are planning to fast and pray, ensure you don't only stay away from food and worldly pleasures, consider more about drawing closer to God in sweet communion, free from sinful lifestyle.
