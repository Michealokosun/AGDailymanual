***FURTHER READING**:* MATT. 13:18-23

> "But the one who received the seed that fell on good soil is the man who hears the word and understands it..." *(Matt. 13:23, NIV).*

***ONE YEAR STUDY PLAN**:* *MATTHEW 24*

In our time, technological advancement is helping preachers and teachers of the Word to reach a wider audience. Gospel messages are aired on the radio, television and the internet. In addition, we now have messages on tape, CD and DVD. We can sit in the comfort of our homes and view broadcasts of special Church programmes in Nigeria, or anywhere around the world.

In spite of the fact that the Word of God is reaching a wider audience through the media, there is a dearth of the Word of God in the lives of many people. Why are people not receiving the word? The parable of the sower answers the question. The first set of people that fail to receive the Word are people who hear the Word and do not understand it. They don't make any effort to explore the Word, creating a chance for the evil one to snatch it away from them.

Secondly, there are those who hear the Word with enthusiasm, but because the Word did not enter their heart, they hardly stand during hard times. Another set, are those who hear the Word, but allow the pursuit of material things and the cares of this life to make the Word unfruitful in them.

Nevertheless, there are those who received the Word. These are people who understand the word and the evidence is seen by the fruits they bear. Hear the Word, understand it and live by it, otherwise, it can never bring increase to us.

***CONTROLLING THOUGHT:***

Understanding the Word brings an increase.

***PRAYER**:*

Lord, help me to receive the word in the good ground of my heart.
